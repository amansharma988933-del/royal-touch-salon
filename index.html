About Royal Touch Salon

Royal Touch Salon is Kalyanpur's destination for refined beauty and grooming. We provide quality beauty and salon services in a comfortable, stylish and welcoming environment – where every guest is treated like royalty.

From precision haircuts and nourishing hair spas to radiant facials and flawless bridal makeup, our skilled professionals use premium products and hygienic practices to ensure you leave feeling confident, relaxed and beautiful.
